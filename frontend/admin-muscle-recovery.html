Recovery & Rest-Day Best Practices

  • Prioritize 7.5 - 9.0 hours of uninterrupted nocturnal sleep for peak growth hormone secretion.
  • Incorporate 15-20 min post-workout infrared sauna to promote peripheral blood flow and metabolite clearance.
  • Hydrate with minimum 3.5 Liters of water daily plus electrolytes on heavy compound lifting days.
  • Schedule 1 to 2 dedicated non-lifting active recovery days every 7-day microcycle.
